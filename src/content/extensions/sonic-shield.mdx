## Intelligent Audio Management

Many websites have drastically different volume levels, forcing you to constantly adjust your system volume slider. `SonicShield` resolves this by automatically applying volume configurations per domain. 

## Web Audio Compression

By utilizing modern Web Audio API compressors, SonicShield dynamically levels out the volume stream:
- **Gain Nodes**: For boosting low signals up to 2x.
- **DynamicsCompressorNode**: To level sudden audio spikes and normalize quiet/loud segments.
- **StereoPannerNode**: For mono conversions.

## Zero Telemetry Policy

SonicShield is built with local security in mind. It does not communicate with external servers or log the videos or songs you stream. Your audio choices stay entirely private.
