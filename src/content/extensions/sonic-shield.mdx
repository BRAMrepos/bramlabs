## Why SonicShield?

Web media volume varies wildly between platforms and video creators. SonicShield injects a lightweight Web Audio compressor node to ensure clean, balanced sound across YouTube, Twitch, podcasts, and social media feeds.

## How It Works

Once enabled, SonicShield monitors audio output levels in active tabs and applies smooth gain correction without distorting voice clarity or music.
