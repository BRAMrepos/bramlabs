## Why Tab Snoozer?

Tab overload slows down your browser and fragments your focus. Tab Snoozer gives you an instant one-click shortcut to park tabs you don't need right now and have them reappear exactly when you're ready to tackle them.

## Key Features

1. **One-Click Snooze Preset**: Quick shortcuts for +1 Hour, Tonight, Tomorrow 9 AM, or Next Monday.
2. **Dashboard Overview**: Manage all currently snoozed tabs in a clean list, wake them early, or group by domain.
3. **Low Overhead**: Lightweight background service worker with zero external analytics scripts.
