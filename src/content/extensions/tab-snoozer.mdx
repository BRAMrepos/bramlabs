## Why Tab Snoozer?

Modern browsers make it easy to open tabs, but hard to close them. Having dozens of open tabs uses excessive memory and clutters your visual focus, leading to **tab anxiety**. 

`Tab Snoozer` resolves this by letting you safely dismiss tabs without losing them. It's like an inbox snooze button, but for your web workspace.

## Privacy by Design

We believe your browsing history is private. Tab Snoozer:
1. Does **not** require login or account creation.
2. Does **not** execute external scripts (no Google Analytics, no trackers).
3. Requests the minimum required permissions (`alarms`, `storage`, and `tabs`).
