## Support Authentic Craft

The web has been flooded with automatically generated e-commerce storefronts. These stores often feature fake reviews, long shipping times, and low-quality goods, undermining legitimate craftsmen and indie stores.

`AI Shop Detector` gives you transparency, helping you make informed decisions before inputting your credit card details.

## Features & Analysis Indicators

- **Domain Registration Check**: Flags sites registered in the last 30 days.
- **Copy-Paste Terms**: Identifies standard template terms of service with placeholder text.
- **Image Metadata**: Searches for reverse-image patterns matching wholesale platforms.
