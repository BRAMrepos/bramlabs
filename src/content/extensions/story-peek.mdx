## Why StoryPeek?

StoryPeek provides a clean, native overlay right inside your Instagram interface, allowing you to watch stories, download media, and manage highlights without leaving the page. It adds simple controls to save stories, reels, posts, and profile media quickly and easily.

## One-Click Media Saver

Save stories, posts, reels, and highlights directly to your device with a single click. StoryPeek downloads media at the maximum resolution provided by the platform.

This provides major advantages:
1. **Instant Access**: No loading queues, third-party server timeouts, or copying links to external helper websites.
2. **High Resolution**: Downloads are saved in their original quality directly to your local folders.
3. **No Setup Required**: Installs directly in your browser and works immediately without requiring custom configurations.

## How it works

Once installed, StoryPeek adds subtle download overlay buttons directly onto Instagram pages and media feeds. You can toggle incognito options, download single stories, or save all active media in one click from the popup interface.
