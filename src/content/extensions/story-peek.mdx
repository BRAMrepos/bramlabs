## Why StoryPeek?

Instagram is designed to track user interactions and report them to content creators. While this helps analytics, it compromises your digital privacy and forces social seen receipts. 

`StoryPeek` gives you control over your digital footprint. By blocking outbound seen telemetry, you can view stories at your own pace without notifying the author.

## Client-Side Interception

Unlike web-based online viewers that require you to paste usernames and fetch data through third-party servers (which violates privacy and frequently gets blocked), StoryPeek works directly inside your browser. It intercepts requests made from your official, authenticated Instagram tab. 

This technique has major advantages:
1. **Zero account risk**: You don't feed your password or session cookies to untrusted third-party services.
2. **Instant access**: No loading queues or server timeouts. It works immediately as you browse.
3. **Download quality**: Downloads are fetched at the highest resolution provided directly by the CDN.

## Security Controls

StoryPeek respects your system security:
- **No Remote Servers**: Zero external API calls, meaning we can't track what accounts you view or download from.
- **Minimal Permissions**: Requests `declarativeNetRequest` to intercept outgoing telemetry headers locally and `downloads` to save media.
- **Open Code**: Designed to be auditable and reviewable at any time.
