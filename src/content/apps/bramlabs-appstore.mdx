## Developer account

BramLabs maintains an **active Amazon Appstore developer account**. Live titles on Fire OS / Vega OS:

Additional titles (Cadence, FocusPulse, GridCraft) are in development.

## Product philosophy

Tools that do one job well. Local-first where possible, low permissions, clear privacy policies, honest feature descriptions.

## Merch connection

Merch on Demand apparel is designed **only** for BramLabs apps, games, extensions, and web tools — product wordmarks, original marks, and studio illustration series. We do not sell third-party brands, sports teams, or copyrighted characters.

## Compliance process

Before any merch publish we:

1. Confirm the design is 100% original BramLabs work
2. Run USPTO trademark screening on distinctive text and marks
3. Review against Amazon Merch Content Policy line by line
