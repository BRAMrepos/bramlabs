## Developer account

BramLabs maintains an active developer account on the Amazon Appstore. Apps are published under the same studio identity used for Chrome extensions and this publisher site.

## Product philosophy

We build tools that do one thing well. Apps follow the same local-first, low-permission principle as our extensions — minimal data collection, clear privacy policies, honest feature descriptions.

## Merch connection

Apparel and branded goods produced under Merch on Demand are designed for **our own** apps and games — product wordmarks, original marks, and studio illustration series. We do not sell third-party brands, sports teams, or copyrighted characters.

## Compliance process

Before any merch publish we:

1. Confirm the design is 100% original BramLabs work
2. Run USPTO trademark screening on all distinctive text and marks
3. Review against Amazon Content Policy line by line
