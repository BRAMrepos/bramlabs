## Overview

Managing irregular work patterns requires more than standard calendar apps. ShiftWorkApp provides specialized scheduling tools built for non-standard work hours:

1. **Rotation Templates**: Easily program 4-on-4-off, DuPont, continental, or custom recurring shift patterns.
2. **Shift Alarms**: Dynamic alarms that automatically adjust wake-up times based on your active shift schedule.
3. **Overtime & Earnings Calculation**: Track hourly rates, shift differentials, and night bonuses locally on your device.
