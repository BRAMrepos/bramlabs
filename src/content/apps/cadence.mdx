## Overview

Cadence is a cross-platform mobile application designed to foster long-term behavioral consistency without intrusive tracking:

1. **Streak Heatmaps**: GitHub-style activity grids that highlight progress over weekly and monthly windows.
2. **Routine Groups**: Chain micro-habits into structured morning, work, and evening routines.
3. **Zero Telemetry**: All habit logs and journal notes are stored securely in local SQLite storage.

## Design Identity

Cadence features a sleek wave rhythm mark and custom typography. Official apparel and stickers carry our original wave motif, checked against USPTO databases prior to publishing.
