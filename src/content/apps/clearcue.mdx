## Status

**Live** on the Amazon Appstore under the BramLabs developer account (Fire OS / Vega OS). Public storefront indexing can take up to 72 hours after go-live — the listing will appear on Amazon search once indexed.

## What it does

ClearCue restores control over mobile focus without blanket app blocking:

1. **Focus sessions** — mute granular channels while keeping emergency calls and VIP alerts open.
2. **Notification batching** — queue social, news, and non-essential alerts into timed digests.
3. **On-device privacy** — processing stays local; notification payloads are not sent to external servers.
