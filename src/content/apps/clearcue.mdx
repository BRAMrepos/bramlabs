## Overview

ClearCue is built to restore control over your mobile focus. Rather than outright blocking apps, it creates an intelligent triage system on your device:

1. **Focus Sessions**: Mute granular channels while keeping emergency calls and VIP notifications open.
2. **Notification Batching**: Queue social, news, and non-essential app alerts into periodic digests (e.g. 12:00 PM and 5:00 PM).
3. **Local Privacy**: 100% on-device processing. No notification payloads are ever sent to external cloud servers.
