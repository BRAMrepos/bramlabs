## Status

**Live** on the Amazon Appstore under the BramLabs developer account (Fire OS / Vega OS). Public storefront indexing can take up to 72 hours after go-live — the listing will appear on Amazon search once indexed.

## What it does

Managing irregular work patterns needs more than a standard calendar:

1. **Rotation templates** — 4-on-4-off, DuPont, continental, or custom recurring patterns.
2. **Shift-aware alarms** — wake times that follow the active rotation.
3. **Overtime & earnings** — track hourly rates, differentials, and night bonuses on device.
