Welcome to the official launch of **BramLabs**!

BramLabs is an independent developer studio focused on building browser extensions that solve real problems without stealing your data.

## The Core Philosophy

Most modern software is bloated, tracking-heavy, and designs interfaces around retention rather than utility. We believe browser tools should:

1. **Do one thing exceptionally well**: No unnecessary features.
2. **Respect User Privacy**: Data should stay on the user's local machine. No external databases, trackers, or marketing pixels.
3. **Be lightning-fast**: Minimal bundle size, efficient CPU cycles, zero lag.

## Our Initial Lineup

We are launching with two tools:

1. **Tab Snoozer**: Declutter your tab bar by scheduling tabs to disappear and wake up when needed.
2. **AI Shop Detector**: Stop buying drop-shipped clones and AI-slop products. Instantly identify non-genuine shops.

We will share deep-dives, engineering retrospectives, and extensions design thoughts on this blog. Stay tuned!
